SEC filing cache identity and corpus recovery

The canonical downloader now addresses each cache entry by the complete SEC accession. It reuses exactly one cached row only when its accession, symbol, exact form and year match the requested filing. Unreadable or mismatched entries fall through to genuine publisher extraction. Original extraction rules, year filters, universe, metadata construction and output schema remain unchanged.

Why the old key was incorrect

The previous filename retained only the first 12 digits after removing accession hyphens. Those digits identify a filing agent and year, not the unique filing sequence. Actual AAPL metadata contains 51 distinct 2020–2025 8-K accessions but only 12 such keys. The retained old cache would incorrectly reuse a different filing for 39 requests. Final output deduplication concealed those repeated records; a successful process exit did not establish complete coverage.

The publisher metadata and retained-file proof records every collision. A live three-filing control returned the third accession incorrectly under the original source and correctly after repair. Replay of the corrected full keys is identical. Controls using real retained records with wrong accession, symbol, form or year are rejected; no invented filing contents or new pytest cases were introduced. The exact red/green control and candidate verification retain scope and source identity.
